Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about adopting through The Purrfect Match

How does the adoption process work?
Our process is simple! Browse available pets, take our compatibility quiz to find your match, then submit an adoption application through our partner shelter (Valley Humane Society). A counselor will review your application and schedule a meet-and-greet. Most adoptions are completed within 3–7 days.
What's included in the adoption fee?
All pets come spayed/neutered, microchipped, and up-to-date on vaccinations. Dogs receive a rabies vaccine, DHPP, and bordetella. Cats receive FVRCP and rabies. A free initial vet checkup within 14 days is also included.
Can I adopt if I live in an apartment?
Absolutely! Many of our pets thrive in apartments. During the matching process, we consider your living space and recommend pets that will be comfortable. Smaller, lower-energy pets often make wonderful apartment companions.
What if the pet doesn't work out?
We want every adoption to be successful, but we understand that sometimes it doesn't work out. Valley Humane Society has a 30-day return policy where you can bring the pet back with no questions asked. We also offer free behavioral consultations to help resolve common adjustment issues.
How accurate is the compatibility quiz?
Our quiz considers your living situation, activity level, experience with pets, and preferences to suggest the best matches. While it's a great starting point, we always recommend meeting the pet in person. Our adoption counselors can also provide personalized guidance!
Are there any requirements to adopt?
You must be at least 18 years old, have valid ID, and provide proof of residence. If you rent, landlord approval for pets is required. We also ask that all current pets be up-to-date on vaccinations. A brief home environment review is part of the process.
Do you have cats and other animals too?
Yes! While our current featured pets are dogs, Valley Humane Society and our partner shelters also have cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, and occasionally other small animals available. Check back often as our listings update regularly.

Through our compatibility matching, community events, and educational resources, we help bridge the gap between adoptable pets and the families who will love them best.

New Pet Parent Tips

Essential advice for making your new pet's transition smooth and happy

🏠

Pet-Proof Your Home

Secure loose wires, remove toxic plants, and put away small objects that could be swallowed. Create a safe space with a bed, water, and toys before your pet arrives.

🕐

Give Them Time to Adjust

The "3-3-3 Rule": 3 days to decompress, 3 weeks to learn your routine, 3 months to feel at home. Be patient — it's a big transition!

🩺

Schedule a Vet Visit

Visit your vet within the first week. Bring adoption paperwork and any medical records from the shelter. Establish a care routine early.

📅

Establish a Routine

Pets thrive on consistency. Set regular times for meals, walks, play, and sleep. A predictable schedule reduces anxiety and builds trust.

🎓

Start Training Early

Use positive reinforcement! Short 5-minute training sessions work best. Focus on basics: sit, stay, come, and leash manners.

❤️

Be Patient with Setbacks

Accidents happen, things get chewed, sleep gets disrupted. It's all normal! Celebrate progress and remember every day gets a little easier.

Adoption Readiness Checklist

Make sure you're prepared before bringing your new friend home! ✓

🏠 Home is pet-proofed (wires secured, toxins removed)
🛏️ Pet bed or crate set up in a quiet area
🍽️ Food & water bowls purchased
🥫 Age-appropriate food (ask shelter for brand recommendations)
🦮 Collar, leash, and ID tag ready
🧸 Toys for enrichment and play
🩺 Vet appointment scheduled within first week
🚗 Secure transport plan (crate or seatbelt harness)
👨‍👩‍👧 All household members on board and prepared
📋 Landlord approval obtained (if renting)
💰 Budget planned for ongoing food, vet, and supplies
🐾 Other pets' vaccinations are current (if applicable)
📖 Read the "New Pet Parent Tips" page
